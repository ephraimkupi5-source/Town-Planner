Town Planner: Make Our Growth Count

You advise the Mayor of Diamond Ridge, a mining town in the Northern Cape.

Town Planner's notebook (your own words)
Glossary

Industrialisation: when a country builds many factories, machines and industries to make goods.

Economic growth: an increase in production, income and GDP.

GDP: the money value of everything a country makes in a year.

Economic development: better life, health, education and fairness for all, as well as more GDP.

Quality of life: the standard of living and well-being of people.

Evaluate: give positives, give negatives, then make a final judgement.
